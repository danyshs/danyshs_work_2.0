Can a lightweight linear-recurrent model match the expressive quality of massive generative transformers in symbolic music continuation?

In our MIREX 2025 paper (*A Traditional Approach to Symbolic Piano Continuation*, co-authored with EleutherAI SOAR), we explored whether architectural efficiency and careful tokenization could beat brute parameter scale.

### The Model: 20M RWKV-7

Rather than scaling up to billions of parameters, our model used a compact 20M-parameter RWKV-7 architecture trained specifically on MIDI token sequences.

The key results under double-blind human listening tests:
- Tied a **39× larger 780M transformer** in subjective continuation quality.
- Outperformed a **60× larger 1.2B parameter model**.
- Maintained strict linear time complexity and constant memory state during generation, enabling low-latency real-time inference on edge devices.

### Takeaways on Efficiency

The results reinforced a core principle in my work: before reaching for massive compute or giant model sizes, verify whether architectural symmetry, data representation, and clean evaluation methodologies can achieve equal or superior results at a fraction of the cost.
